# Combat acceptance test

Launch the game locally and start a new run on Hard, the default. Use short fire presses when counting hits. Collect an issue whenever you need to refill energy.

| Try this | Expected result |
|---|---|
| Watch a UFO from outside its patrol range. Approach within about 26 metres, move sideways, then retreat out of range. | The UFO patrols until Chomp approaches, then closes in, circles and fires red bolts roughly every 1.4 seconds. Once Chomp is out of range, it returns to its patrol. |
| Approach an evil chompbot on open ground and move around it. | It strafes while facing Chomp and fires red bolts roughly every 1.1 seconds. |
| Land ten individual laser hits on a UFO and on a chompbot. | Both survive the first nine hits. Each tenth hit destroys its target and awards 100 points. |
| Collect all ten issues and watch the Alien Overlord emerge. Land hits and watch its health bar. | The boss starts at 300 health, moves sideways after emerging, and cycles through its spread, ring and tracking burst. It fires more often at half health and below. The portal opens only when the bar reaches zero. |
| Hold fire until the bar shows 100% Laser rain, then release from open ground with hills in view and move away. | Four distinct waves fall over the release point, with 72 beams per wave out to about 72 metres. Each wave mixes red, white and blue beams. |
| From a high hill, fire single, big, spread and circle shots across open air. | Clear shots travel up to 100 metres, half the map width. Hits on cover still stop immediately. |
| Hold Cross on a DualShock (or Ctrl on a keyboard) while moving on flat ground, then release it. Press Cross beside a sign. | Chomp moves at double walking speed while held and returns to walking speed on release. Chomp no longer turns to face the camera, and the sign still displays its text. |
| Aim with the right stick, then try left-stick aiming with L2 and sniper aiming with R2. Try the arrow keys and mouse too. | Aiming is 30% slower than before in each mode. L2 still locks movement and R2 still magnifies 5×. |
| Start fresh runs on Medium and Easy, count hits and observe enemies at the edge of their engagement ranges. | Regular enemies take five hits on Medium and two on Easy. The boss starts at 150 and 60 health respectively. Enemies fire half as often on Medium and one-fifth as often on Easy. UFOs and bots engage at half or one-fifth of their Hard ranges. |
| On Hard, clear the portal and choose **Start Level 2**. Clear it again and choose **Start Level 3**. | Each level starts with fresh issues, full energy and the previous score. Level 2 has 12-hit enemies and a 360-hit boss. Level 3 has 14-hit enemies and a 432-hit boss. Firing rates and engagement ranges grow 20% each level. |
| Shoot into a cliff or hillside with an enemy on the other side, then let a red bolt hit the same cover. Step into the open and try both again. | Both shots stop at the obstacle and leave the target unharmed. Direct hits in the open damage the enemy or Chomp. |
| Shoot a moon rock and the glowing moonbase target from clear angles. | A shot still breaks the rock. Ten hits to the target still free the trapped Chomps. |
